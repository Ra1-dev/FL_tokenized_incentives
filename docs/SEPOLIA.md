# Public-testnet demonstration

Generated by `python scripts/sepolia_demo.py` from `results/sepolia/demo.json`.

The unchanged reward pool ran three complete rounds on Sepolia (chain id 11155111) on 2026-11-27: two honest clients and one zero-update free-rider registered with stake, committed and revealed update digests, received coordinator-posted scores, settled with vesting, and claimed matured rewards. The free-rider was slashed in round 3 after three zero-quality participations (threshold 0, patience 3). After every round the pool's stakes, claimable balances, unvested tranches and wallets matched an independent integer model of the settlement order.

The recorded gas figures therefore describe the Cancun schedule; a network that prices state creation higher charges more and fits fewer participants under the per-transaction cap.

The demonstration shows the same bytecode settling on a public network; the coordinator remains a trusted score source, as in the threat model.
